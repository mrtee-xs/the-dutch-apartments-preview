The Duch Standard

Where Executive Comfort Meets Residential Sanctuary.

The Duch Apartments provides discerning guests with a peaceful, secure sanctuary in the heart of Aguda, Surulere. Whether you are traveling for business across mainland Lagos or the islands, returning from the diaspora, or booking a private weekend getaway, our apartments combine the independence of private luxury home living with hotel-grade reliability.

Each apartment is furnished with high-speed fiber internet, smart entertainment, a fully fitted open-plan kitchen, and dedicated workspace. With dual generator power backup and 24-hour on-site security, your stay is seamless, comfortable, and completely protected.

  • Guaranteed 24/7 uninterrupted electricity with dual automatic backup generators
  • 24/7 gated security, professional guards, CCTV monitoring, and secure on-site parking
  • Unlimited high-speed fiber Wi-Fi and Smart TVs with Netflix and premium DStv channels
  • Fully equipped self-catering culinary kitchens with refrigerator, microwave, and cookware
